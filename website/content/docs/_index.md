Welcome to sobarch's documentation. The name comes from the Korean
*sobak* (소박, simple) fused with *arch*, and doubles as "sober arch": a
plain, reproducible take on Arch Linux and Hyprland, without the
ceremony.

## Where to start

- **Installing:** the getting-started and installer sections walk
  through the install, disk layout, snapshots, rescue media, and
  software profiles.
- **Using the desktop:** the desktop and shell sections cover the menu
  system, keybindings, theming, and keeping your config up to date.
- **Packages:** how AUR and custom packages are vendored, built, and
  updated without an AUR helper.
- **Reference:** the security baseline, the `sobarch` command (under Desktop), where
  files and logs live, troubleshooting, and the FAQ.
