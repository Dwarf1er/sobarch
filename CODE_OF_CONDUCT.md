# Code of Conduct

Sobarch is a small project, and it stays useful by being a place where
people can ask questions, report problems and send changes without being
made to feel unwelcome.

## What we expect

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly when something is unclear.
- Keep to the topic: issues and discussions are for the project.
- Accept that a project with one maintainer has to say no sometimes.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and
block people who break these rules.

To report a problem, email **contact@antoinepoulin.com**. Reports are
kept confidential.

This applies in the repository, its issues, pull requests and
discussions, and anywhere else someone is representing the project.
